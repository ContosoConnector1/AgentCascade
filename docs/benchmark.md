# 500-account benchmark

The headline quality measure for private preview (AC-27).

- **Sample:** 500 accounts, stratified by segment + renewal stage.
- **Labels:** 2 reviewers per item; track inter-rater agreement.
- **Metric:** Cohen's kappa of Cascade drafts vs. labels. **Floor: kappa >= 0.70.**
- **Schedule:** re-baselined to **early July** at the Sprint 1 readiness call.
- **Rule:** no public quantitative claim until this lands (launch + 2). We hold kappa >= 0.7 over raw speed.
